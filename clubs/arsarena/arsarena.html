Arsarena - The Arts and Editing Club

🎨 About Us

“There are three responses to a piece of design – yes, no, and WOW! Wow is the one to aim for.”
And our Arsarena the arts and editing club aims for it. We aim to project hidden talents of art in digital or paperback form and to encourage students to showcase or develop new creative skills.
“Design can be art. Design can be aesthetics. Design is so simple, that’s why it is so complicated.”

✨ Vision

👉 To project the hidden talent of art and to showcase the splendid talents either in digital form or paperback.

🎯 Mission

👉 To encourage students who are truly skilled and are looking out for opportunities to showcase their skills and also to help students who are passionate to learn new skills.
