About

The Enigma is one of the active clubs run by the institute's students from 2022-23. It organizes many dance events, workshops, etc., at SPEC throughout the year to ensure students stay engaged creatively. Formed to unite students with a passion for dance and represent the college through performance.

Mission

  • ✅ Display understanding of expressions and rhythm.
  • ✅ Apply value systems and principles to performances.
  • ✅ Provide training to develop dancing skills.
  • ✅ Participate in institutional-level programs.
  • ✅ Compete in intercollege, state, and national competitions.
