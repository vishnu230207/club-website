About

Rotaract is an international service organization whose stated purpose is to bring together business and professional leaders to provide humanitarian service and to advance goodwill and peace around the world.

Members of Rotaract develop leadership, public speaking, social, business, personal, and vocational skills as well as improve cultural awareness.

Rotarians believe in service above self, and it is richly rewarding. It is the best chance to do something for somebody else.

“Alone we can do so little, together we can do much.”
