Awake Revelation - The Music Club

🎶 Motto

The club strives to promote all student participation in the contribution and enjoyment of their abilities/talents and to provide an outlet for all interested in music. The club also offers practice spaces and wide opportunities for students to come together to perform and showcase their talents at events throughout the year.

🎼 Intro

The club welcomes members who are good vocalists and instrumentalists. The club students actively participate in the events and the club consists of members like:

  • PRESIDENT
  • CLUB ADMIN
  • CONTENT CREATOR
  • EDITOR
  • VOCALIST
  • INSTRUMENTALISTS
    • Guitarist
    • Drummer
    • Keyboard
