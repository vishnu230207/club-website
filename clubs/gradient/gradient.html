Intro

The Gradient Club conducts interactive sessions mainly for AI & ML students to assist them in discovering or deepening their domain of interest. Students can explore multiple tech areas before deciding their direction.

Vision

To be renowned for molding students into skilled professionals fostering technological development, research, and innovative ideas that meet societal needs.

Mission

  • 👉 Making students knowledgeable in AI and its applied areas.
  • 👉 Training students in cutting-edge technologies with community support.
  • 👉 Fostering research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
