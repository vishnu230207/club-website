About

👉 Radio club is among the top clubs in St. Peter’s Engineering college.
👉 This club entitles its team to bring out their talents through RJ, Content, Creative, Audio editing, Technical, E-publishing & Design.

Motto

The main motto of the Radio club is to provide infotainment by playing debates, monologues, talks on lifestyle, education, and more.
