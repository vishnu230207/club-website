elITians - The Social Media Club

🚀 Vision

The main purpose of the elITians club is to become a front-runner in preparing innovators and making them competent professionals by enabling them to take up any kind of challenges in the Information Technology industry.

🎯 Mission

Our club:

  • 👉 Enhances students' knowledge of the latest technologies.
  • 👉 Organizes and participates in technical programs.
  • 👉 Provides forums to exhibit students' creative talents.
  • 👉 Aims to give everyone the power to create and share ideas and information without barriers.

💡 Values

  • 👉 We believe in working and learning together.
  • 👉 We believe in integrity and working towards excellence.
  • 👉 We inspire discovery and invention through play.

📅 Events Conducted

The Department of Information Technology of St. Peter’s Engineering College inaugurated a Student Association Cell on 11/12/2021 with the name elITians.

Under this club, various activities were conducted during 2021–2022 to develop technical and interpersonal skills among students. This club is headed by Mrs. Madhavi Karumudi, Assistant Professor.
