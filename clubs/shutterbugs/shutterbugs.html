Shutter Bugs - The Photography Club

🎯 Motto

Pause moments & capture memories. To capture memories for the college and to the students.

📌 Objectives

  • ✅ Capture memories of college life and events.
  • ✅ Teach photography skills from basics to advanced.
  • ✅ Organize workshops and training sessions.
  • ✅ Motivate participation in inter and intra-college contests.
  • ✅ Cover almost every college event with photography support.

📷 About

Photography is more than just a hobby—it’s a way to express joy and preserve memories. Shutter Bugs, founded on 12th August 2013, is one of the most active and prestigious clubs at SPEC. It started with just 7 students and has grown to over 50 passionate photographers.

The club provides a platform for students to learn photography and videography skills, receive hands-on training, and use real equipment. Students of all experience levels are welcomed and trained to become skilled photographers.

Shutter Bugs captures every major college event including Independence Day, Graduation Day, Batukamma, AQUILLA, Musical Fest, Workshops, and many more.
